Getting to Taniti

Your guide to reaching our beautiful island

Arrival Options

  • Air Travel
  • Cruise Ship

Air Travel Details

Most visitors arrive by air. Taniti is served by a small airport that can accommodate small jets and propeller planes. The island is currently expanding the airport so larger jets will be able to land within the next few years.

Cruise Ship Information

A small cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay once per week for an overnight stay. Visitors arriving by cruise ship can explore Taniti City, beaches, and local attractions during their visit.

Visitor Tips

  • Plan ahead for holiday closures
  • Currency: USD, euros, and yen accepted
  • English widely spoken in Taniti City
  • Airport expansion may affect travel schedules
