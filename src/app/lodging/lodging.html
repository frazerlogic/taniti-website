About Taniti Lodging

Taniti offers a wide variety of lodging options ranging from inexpensive hostels to a large four-star resort. Many small, family-owned hotels and a growing number of bed and breakfasts provide comfortable stays for visitors. All lodging is strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.
