Getting Around Taniti

Explore the island with convenient transportation options

Public Transportation

Public buses serve Taniti City and run daily from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. Private buses operate throughout the rest of the island.

Rental Options

  • Rental Cars (near the airport)
  • Bikes (helmets required by law)
  • Car Share Services

Taxis & Shuttles

  • Taxis available in Taniti City
  • Hotel Shuttles
  • Cruise Ship Shuttle (weekly)

Walkability

Taniti City is flat and very walkable. Many tourists stay near Merriton Landing, which is easy to explore on foot and close to beaches, shops, and entertainment.
