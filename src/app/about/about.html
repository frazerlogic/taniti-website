Island Overview

Taniti is a small tropical island under 500 square miles, featuring sandy and rocky beaches, lush rainforests, a safe harbor, and a mountainous interior with an active volcano. The island is home to about 20,000 residents, with a growing tourism industry.

Restaurants

Taniti has 10 restaurants: five serving local fish and rice, three offering American-style meals, and two specializing in Pan-Asian cuisine.

Grocery Stores

The island has two supermarkets, two smaller grocery stores, and one 24-hour convenience store.

Transportation

Most visitors arrive by air, though a small cruise ship docks weekly. Taniti’s airport supports small jets and propeller planes, with expansion underway to accommodate larger jets.

Ground Transportation

  • Public buses (5 a.m. – 11 p.m.)
  • Private buses
  • Taxis
  • Rental cars (near the airport)
  • Bikes (helmets required)
  • Walkable city layout
  • Merriton Landing is easily explored on foot

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Power outlets are 120 volts (same as the U.S.).
  • Alcohol cannot be sold or served between midnight and 9:00 a.m.
  • The drinking age is 18, and enforcement is relaxed.
  • English is widely spoken in Taniti City; rural areas speak little English.
  • The island has one hospital and several clinics with multilingual staff.
  • Violent crime is rare; petty crime is increasing with tourism.
  • Many attractions and restaurants close on national holidays.
  • Currency: USD is standard, but euros and yen are accepted.
  • Banks offer currency exchange; major credit cards are widely accepted.
